Reference-project gallery

Bodies worth building.

A working shelf of AISocket projects: small, reproducible machines and interfaces that turn the architecture into something a second person can actually build, test and criticize.

Status matters: these cards are not claims of finished products. They are build targets. Photos, schematics, 3D models, BOMs, firmware and video instructions will replace the provisional illustrations as the projects are completed.
planned buildrobotics

Optical Peripheral Nervous System

One continuous plastic-optical-fiber backbone feeds a small number of local controller nodes. Each node can deterministically serve several motors, encoders and sensors while the central controller sends higher-level motion state.

The experimental tap is a clamp-on controlled bend with an optical collector. Nodes answer in scheduled slots; a mandatory empty/alive reply makes silence diagnostically meaningful. A node may also become a master for the next segment.

Server → POF backbone → ~10 nodes → ~10 motors/node
planned buildaccess

Garage Gate Body

A bounded access-control body that can combine plate recognition, vehicle appearance, owner proximity and face confirmation without giving the invited intelligence raw authority over the actuator.

The gate keeps hard interlocks, local emergency behavior and actuator limits. AISocket exposes observations, identity evidence and the permitted open/close action.

planned buildvoice

Voice Secretary

A local voice body for reminders, messages, household actions and structured interaction with external intelligence while routine skills remain local and inspectable.

The reference build should show microphone/speaker hardware, the passport, permissions, offline behavior and clear separation between conversational interpretation and deterministic actions.

reference demoESP32

Phone + ESP32 Crawler

A small mobile body that demonstrates the simplest AISocket loop: a phone provides rich sensing and connectivity, an ESP32 owns local motor behavior, and intelligence receives a bounded interface rather than raw pins.

A useful first public demo because it is inexpensive, visually obvious and easy for another builder to reproduce.

planned buildvision + audio

Home Camera + Audio Body

A household sensing body built around cameras, microphones and local event logic. AISocket defines exactly what can be observed, what can be retained, and what actions an authorized participant may request.

The build is intended as a concrete test of privacy boundaries, local preprocessing and the flight-recorder model.

planned buildbrowser

Browser MCU Workshop

A browser-first workshop for selecting a supported board, editing firmware and passport data, compiling, flashing, opening telemetry, running self-test and inspecting traces.

The browser is the workshop, not the safety boundary: hard limits, watchdogs and emergency behavior stay in the device.

research buildinvestigation

OpenPCB Investigator

A two-sided board investigation machine exposing cameras, probes, height measurement and controlled electrical experiments as bounded AISocket tools.

Its purpose is not to guess a schematic from appearance alone, but to let intelligence choose discriminating physical tests and preserve the evidence trail.

planned integrationfactory

CNC / Robot-Arm Integration

A reference industrial body showing how a conventional motion controller, servo drives, encoders, interlocks and diagnostics can be exposed to AISocket without moving the servo loop into the language model.

The optical nervous-system project can eventually become one hardware option inside this larger reference build.

planned buildutility

Water Utility Body

A small infrastructure example combining meter state, valves, alarms and service procedures under explicit permissions and local fail-safe behavior.

It is useful because the physical actions are simple while provenance, diagnostics, offline behavior and authorization are not.

planned buildsoftware body

RS.ge Accounting Assistant

A non-robotic AISocket body demonstrating that the same passport / mandate / trace pattern also applies to software. Routine bookkeeping stays deterministic; interpretation is invited only where needed.

A strong counterexample to the idea that AISocket is only about motors: a body may be a bounded software environment as well as a machine.
